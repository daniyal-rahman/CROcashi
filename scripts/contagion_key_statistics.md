## Interpretation Benchmarks

### What Does Contagion Rate Mean?

```
< 30%:  Low contagion - terminations largely independent
30-50%: Moderate contagion - some competitive dynamics
50-70%: High contagion - strong competitive/scientific signaling
> 70%:  Very high contagion - indication-wide challenges
> 90%:  Extreme contagion - systemic barriers affecting all players
```

### Company Bellwether Status

```
Top tier (Pfizer, Novartis, AstraZeneca):
```

## Investment & Strategic Thresholds

### Red Flags (High Contagion Risk)

```
□ Indication contagion rate > 70%
□ 3+ recent competitor terminations in past 12 months
□ Bellwether company (Pfizer, Novartis, etc.) recently exited
□ Phase 2 program in crowded indication (10+ competitors)
□ Similar mechanism to recently failed programs
□ Funding round raised < 18 months ago (insufficient runway)
```

### Green Flags (Lower Contagion Risk)

```
□ Indication contagion rate < 40%
□ Novel mechanism (no prior failures in indication)
□ Biomarker-selected population (differentiated from competitors)
□ Only 2-4 competitors in indication
□ Phase 1 or very early Phase 2 (more flexibility)
□ 24+ months cash runway (can weather funding drought)
```

## Quick Decision Framework

### When Competitor Fails, Ask:

```
1. Mechanism differentiation?
   □ Yes, fundamentally different → Continue (monitor closely)
   □ No, similar/same → STRONG EXIT SIGNAL

2. Patient population differentiation?
   □ Yes, biomarker/stage selected → Continue (monitor closely)
   □ No, same population → EXIT SIGNAL

3. How many competitors failed?
   □ 1-2 failures → Assess differentiation
   □ 3-4 failures → Strong exit signal
   □ 5+ failures → IMMEDIATE EXIT SIGNAL

4. What's your phase?
   □ Phase 1 → More flexibility to continue
   □ Phase 2 → Critical decision point
   □ Phase 3 → Too late to pivot, assess completion risk

5. Funding runway?
   □ 24+ months → Can weather storm
   □ 12-24 months → Vulnerable to funding contagion
   □ < 12 months → HIGH RISK
```
